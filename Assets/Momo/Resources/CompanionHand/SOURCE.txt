four weights per vertex. Original 1024px diffuse UV texture retained.
Left and right prefabs are baked separately with corrected normals, winding and bind poses. No runtime negative scale. Runtime authors food-offer/finger-curl
and petting poses; not motion capture. Model is not claimed to be newly authored.
